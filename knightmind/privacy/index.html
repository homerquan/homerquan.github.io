KnightMind App Support

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Data collection

KnightMind does not collect or transmit personal or gameplay data. Chess play, coaching, and speech run on your device. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it does not use your microphone.

Storage and deletion

KnightMind saves the current game's moves and preferences, such as board view, difficulty, game mode, and spoken commentary, in its local app storage. Starting a new game replaces the saved moves. Deleting the app removes its local app data from your device.

External links and support

When you open a project link from Settings, it opens in your browser and the destination's privacy policy applies. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages.

GitHub Privacy Statement

If you email support, we use your email address and the information you choose to share to respond to your request. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of support correspondence, email homerquan@gmail.com.
